About me

I started in telecom and Linux operations: Red Hat servers, Oracle databases, networks and middleware. Today I lead a DevOps team working across AWS, Google Cloud and Azure.

I love the detective side of the job: ruling out possibilities one by one until I find the missing piece, even when it turns out to be one key press at the right moment. I also love making the right way the easy way, with shared modules and pipeline templates so every new project starts secure from day one.

I use AI every day to think through ideas, write scripts and troubleshoot faster. I still own the thinking: I understand the problem, decide the goal, and check the result.
